About

A simple class seating-plan composer. Imports student names and photos directly from a SEQTA class-roll PDF, generates randomised or alphabetical layouts, supports separation rules between students, exports to PNG, CSV, or print.

Built 03 May 2026 by Barry Vayler. Feedback and ideas welcome — please get in touch!

Settings

Seating constraints — students who must not sit near each other when the grid is shuffled.
Tip: also opens with Ctrl+Shift+K. Room reference, columns and aisles live in the left sidebar.

Keep apart
Add lines of students who must not sit near each other. Each line is a separation group: every student on the same line will be placed at least two seats away from the others on that line (no horizontal, vertical, or diagonal neighbours), enforced when you press Shuffle. Aisles are visual only — students seated either side of an aisle still count as neighbours.
